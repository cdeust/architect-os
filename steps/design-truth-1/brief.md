# Step V.1: the design of the platform and the slide of the isolation say what the tree holds

## The fact

`docs/design/platform-v3-srt.md` is the design of the platform, written on 30 October 2026 and amended since. It is a design document, not a journal. A sweep by a second reader of another provider, read-only, found about thirty passages in the present tense that the tree contradicts, of three kinds: a decision that was reversed and whose text stayed (containers after decisions 21 and 22; session hooks after decision 2); a design still in force and not built, written as if it existed (three meta-tools, the executor, the path of an `execute` call); a description that drifted (every internal link as MCP on stdio while a module calls the gateway back over HTTP; « no sandbox reads the secrets of the home » wider than what the profiles name; « the killed group leaves no descendant » against an accepted risk that documents a macOS case). The slide `docs/communication/deck/slides/isolation.html` carries one sentence of the same kind. The step before (journal, section 104) named this as the next step.

A document of design that states as present what is not, or no longer, the case is a false statement of architecture and, where it speaks of isolation, of security.

## What to do

The sweep is below, as its author wrote it. It ran nothing: **each of its findings is a claim to verify, not an instruction.** For each:

1. Read the passage, and read the code, the profile, the scenario, the decision or the commit the finding rests on.
2. Decide which of the three kinds it is, and write accordingly, in the French of the document and in its style:
   - **a reversed decision**: the passage says what holds today and names the decision that reversed it, with its date; the earlier state stays as one dated clause when it explains a later choice, and goes otherwise;
   - **a design in force, not built**: the passage says so in so many words (what the tree does today; what the brick will add, and which brick), and you check that the roadmap of `README.md` (« What comes next ») carries the brick; if it does not, say so in the report and do not invent a row;
   - **a description that drifted**: the passage says what the code does, no more.
3. Where the finding is wrong, leave the text and say why, with the file and line that show it.
4. A statement corrected in one place is corrected in every place of the tree where it is made: for each correction, search `README.md`, `docs/` (outside the two journals), `CLAUDE.md`, the features and the comments, and list the places.

## Limits

- The tables of decisions and of accepted risks, and every dated account of what was decided or measured, are history: they are not rewritten. A numbered decision is never renumbered nor removed; if a decision of the table was reversed and the table does not say so, add the mention to its row and nothing else.
- The JSON block of section 5 is the template proposed on 30 October 2026: it stays, labelled as such.
- `docs/design/journal.md` and `bench/JOURNAL.md` are append-only and are not edited; `steps/` is never edited; `docs/design/orchestration-fable-5-2.md` is not touched.
- No code, no profile, no scenario changes in this step. If a passage is true only if the code changed, that is a finding for the maintainer: report it, do not change the code.
- The format check of the repository leaves `docs/design/` out (`.prettierignore`): do not reformat what you do not change. Say in the report whether the two documents of design you edit pass `npx prettier --check --ignore-path /dev/null` before and after, and do not fix what was already failing.
- Nothing of a workstation, an account, a plan or a private path is written.
